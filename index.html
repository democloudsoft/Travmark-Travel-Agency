Terms & Conditions

Important guidelines governing booking enquiries and travel services through Travmark.

1. Booking Requests & Enquiries

Submitting an online form through this website constitutes an inquiry and does not guarantee ticket issuance or hotel room reservation until verified and confirmed by Travmark Travel Agency.

2. Pricing & Availability

Airfares, hotel rates, and package estimates fluctuate according to season, airline fuel surcharges, currency exchange rates, and availability. Final pricing is confirmed at the time of ticket issuance or payment.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Airlines, hotels, and ground transport operators are independent suppliers. Their individual carriage contracts, check-in deadlines, baggage rules, and cancellation terms apply to your journey.

4. Travel Documentation & Visas

Travelers are responsible for ensuring that their passports hold at least six months of remaining validity, and that they possess appropriate entry visas, transit visas, and health certifications required by destination authorities.

5. Cancellations & Modifications

Change fees, refund policies, and cancellation charges are determined by the relevant airlines and hotels. Travmark Travel Agency processes change and refund requests according to these third-party supplier rules.
